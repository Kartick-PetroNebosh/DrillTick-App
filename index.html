Drilling & Well Control
DRILLING & WELL CONTROL

Kill Sheet Calculators

  • IWCF-style Surface (8 calculations) and Subsea (9)
  • KMW, ICP, FCP, K100 and a step-down pressure schedule
  • Choke-line friction and initial dynamic casing pressure
  • Rounded in the safe direction; closely matching A4 PDF

SCR Measurement

  • Slow-rate pump pressures for surface and subsea wells
  • Pump displacement and riser margin pressure
  • Embedded BOP and mud-pump simulator on each screen

Simulators & Training Tools

  • MWD Survey: release torque, pump off, wait, restart, read survey
  • Downlinking: RPM modulation, mud flow and wired pipe
  • Rig Sensors: hook load, drawworks, pump strokes, SPP
  • BOP, mud pump and driller’s console (surface and subsea)

Save Slots & Local Database

  • Numbered slots with a label and time for sheets and permits
  • Versioned database that only adds tables on upgrade
  • Works offline, with no accounts and no cloud
Safety & Lifting Modules
SAFETY & LIFTING MODULES

Permit to Work

  • Hot, Cold, Confined Space, Electrical, Height, Excavation
  • One five-section form: hazards, gas testing, checklist, authorisation
  • Composite PDF bundling permits and lift plans

Checklists & Inspections

  • Orientation, Pre-Spud, Drops, SIMOPS Programme and Matrix
  • Safety Audit, Mast & Substructure, Contractor Audit
  • Single-tap statuses, custom rows and PDF export

Reporting & Dashboard

  • Incident, Near Miss and Observation forms with a risk meter
  • Automatic High Potential Incident check
  • Seven-page dashboard and an HSE monthly report

Lifting Operations

  • Crane plan: load chart, % capacity, ground pressure, tandem lifts
  • Rigging register with inspections and next-due dates
  • Sling angle, tension and factor-of-safety calculations
